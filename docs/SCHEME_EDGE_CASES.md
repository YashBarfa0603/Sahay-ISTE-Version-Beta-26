# SAHAY (सहाय) — Grounded Scheme Edge Cases & Important Precautions

## 1. Executive Summary

A critical failure mode for citizen welfare applications across India is **silent disqualification** due to complex statutory exclusions, unadvertised cutoff dates, and banking mismatch technicalities. 

SAHAY mitigates this through a dedicated **"Edge Cases & Important Precautions"** section prominently integrated into the **Overview tab** of every scheme detail view. This documentation outlines the authoritative legal and procedural rules extracted from official scheme operational guidelines.

## 2. Scheme-Specific Grounded Disqualification Rules

### 2.1 PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- **Cutoff Date**: Landholding ownership must be registered in state revenue records (RoR/Khatauni) **before February 1, 2019**. Land purchased or transferred after this cutoff is disqualified (except via succession/inheritance).
- **Family Unit Definition**: Defined as Husband, Wife, and minor children. Only **one** entitlement (₹6,000/year in 3 installments) is permitted per family unit. Multiple claims within the same household trigger automated recovery and DBT blockages.
- **Statutory Exclusions**:
  - Institutional landholders (temple trusts, registered societies, corporate farming).
  - Any family member holding or having held a constitutional post (Minister, MP, MLA, Mayor, Zila Panchayat President).
  - Serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Govts, Ministries, PSUs, and Autonomous Bodies (except Class IV / Group D / Multi-Tasking Staff).
  - Pensioners receiving ₹10,000 or more per month (excluding Group D).
  - Any family member who filed or paid Income Tax in the preceding assessment year.
  - Practicing registered professionals: Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects.
  - Agricultural land converted for commercial, non-agricultural, or residential real estate development.

### 2.2 PM-JAY (Ayushman Bharat — Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana)
- **Deprivation Basis**: Entitlement is determined by Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC 2011) deprivation criteria (D1–D7 rural, 11 occupational categories urban) or active NFSA ration card lists.
- **Ayushman Vaya Vandana**: Senior citizens aged 70+ receive universal top-up cover of ₹5,00,000 irrespective of income or SECC status.
- **Family Size and Pre-existing Illness**: There is strictly **no cap on family size or age**, and all pre-existing medical conditions are covered from day one of card generation.
- **Disqualifications**:
  - Pucca house with 3 or more rooms.
  - Ownership of motorised fishing boats or agricultural machinery above statutory horsepower limits.
  - Households with government employees or income tax filers (unless qualified under state-specific expanded Ayushman lists).

### 2.3 PMAY-G (Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana — Gramin)
- **Primary Disqualification**: Ownership of a permanent (pucca) dwelling anywhere in India by any member of the household.
- **Asset Exclusions**:
  - Households owning any motorized two-wheeler, three-wheeler, four-wheeler, or motorized boat.
  - Households possessing a mechanized three- or four-wheeler agricultural vehicle.
  - Households with a Kisan Credit Card (KCC) credit limit of ₹50,000 or higher.
  - Households with any member serving as a government employee (regular, contractual, or retired).
  - Households running a non-agricultural enterprise registered with the government.
  - Households paying professional tax or income tax.
  - Landholding of 2.5 acres or more of irrigated land with at least one irrigation equipment.

### 2.4 Ujjwala 2.0 (Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana)
- **Household Restriction**: Only adult women belonging to eligible disadvantaged categories (SC/ST, PMAY beneficiaries, Antyodaya Anna Yojana, Most Backward Classes, Forest Dwellers, Tea Garden Tribes) may apply.
- **No Prior Connection**: There must be **no existing active LPG connection** in the name of the applicant or any member of the applicant's ration card household from any Oil Marketing Company (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL).
- **Migration Concession**: Migrant families do not require a permanent domicile certificate; a self-declaration of address and family composition is accepted under Ujjwala 2.0.

### 2.5 Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme (PMSS)
- **Category Limitation**: Open exclusively to dependent wards and widows of deceased or retired Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF), Assam Rifles, Railway Protection Force (RPF), and Armed Forces personnel.
- **Academic Benchmark**: Minimum 60% marks in the qualifying Minimum Educational Qualification (MEQ, i.e., 10+2 or Diploma).
- **Course Type**: Valid strictly for the **first professional degree** (e.g., MBBS, B.Tech, BDS, B.Pharm, BBA/BCA). Lateral entry, master's degrees (except MBA/MCA where approved), and distance/correspondence courses are disqualified.

### 2.6 Mukhyamantri Ladli Behna Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)
- **Age and Residence**: Woman must be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh aged between 21 and 60 years on January 1 of the calendar year.
- **Income Ceiling**: Annual family income from all sources must not exceed ₹2,50,000.
- **Agricultural Land Ceiling**: Family must own **less than 5 acres** of cultivable land. Ownership of 5 acres or more constitutes immediate disqualification.
- **Vehicle Prohibition**: Family must **not own any four-wheeler** vehicle (tractors used for agricultural work are exempt).
- **Individual Account Mandate**: The disbursement bank account must be an **individual, single account** in the applicant woman's name. Joint accounts or accounts in the husband's name are strictly rejected.

## 3. Universal Technical & Application Precautions

1. **Aadhaar vs. Bank Record Spelling Match**:
   - Any character or whitespace discrepancy between the citizen's Aadhaar name, bank passbook, and land title halts automated Public Financial Management System (PFMS) verification.
2. **NPCI DBT Seeding**:
   - Having a bank account is insufficient; the account must be explicitly seeded on the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) mapper for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
3. **Mobile Number Integrity**:
   - The registered mobile number must remain active and identical across Aadhaar UIDAI records, bank KYC, and scheme application portals.
4. **Anti-Fraud & Zero Intermediary Fees**:
   - Government welfare portals (pmkisan.gov.in, beneficiary.nha.gov.in, etc.) and CSC centers charge only prescribed statutory nominal fees or are completely free. Citizens are warned against paying unauthorized agents or touts.
